# halting problem

Type: CONCEPT

## From [dynamics-of-the-unpredictable-micro03](/entities/dynamics-of-the-unpredictable-micro03.md) (2026-06-09)
- A mathematical proof establishing the impossibility of a generalized algorithm predicting if a program will halt.
- Inherited by physical systems that encode Turing completeness.
- Related to undecidability.

## From [dynamics-of-the-unpredictable-micro04](/entities/dynamics-of-the-unpredictable-micro04.md) (2026-06-09)
- Calculating the spectral gap of a material is equivalent to the halting problem.
- It is universally undecidable.
