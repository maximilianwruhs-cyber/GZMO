# Dice Mechanics (GZMO Attractor Dice)

Type: CONCEPT

In the GZMO architecture, `/dice` is the canonical reference implementation of a **Coupled Mechanical Skill (CCL-3)**. Rather than relying on standard pseudorandom number generators (PRNGs), rolls are deterministically derived from the current coordinates and phase states of the Lorenz attractor.

## Chaos-Derived Rolls
Rolls are generated by computing a high-frequency fractional component from the Lorenz snapshot coordinates (`x`, `y`, `z`), the effective `chaos_val`, and the active `tick`:

$$\text{combined} = \text{fract}(\text{chaos\_val} \times 10000.0 + |x| \times 100.0 + |y| \times 10.0 + |z|)$$
$$\text{roll} = \lfloor \text{combined} \times \text{max\_sides} \rfloor + 1$$

This ensures that consecutive rolls reflect the attractor's exact state in phase space.

## Variant Selection
To provide narrative diversity, a variant is selected using a XOR hash of the coordinates and the active tick:

$$\text{variant} = (\lfloor |x| \times 1000.0 \rfloor \oplus \lfloor |y| \times 1000.0 \rfloor \oplus \text{tick}) \pmod{\text{pool\_size}}$$

- **D20** uses 5 variants per face (100 total outcomes).
- **D6** uses 3 variants per face (18 total outcomes).

## Mechanical Tier Triggers
Each roll results in downstream feedback events that alter the system's `tension` ($\tau$), `energy` ($\epsilon$), or crystallize specific `ThoughtSeed` items:

- **Catastrophic Fail (D20 Roll 1)**: $+10.0$ Tension, $-5.0$ Energy, `dice_catastrophe` seed.
- **Legendary Success (D20 Roll 20)**: $-5.0$ Tension, $+15.0$ Energy, `dice_legendary` seed.
- **Snake Eyes (D6 Roll 1)**: $+5.0$ Tension, $-3.0$ Energy, `dice_catastrophe` seed.
- **Perfect D6 (D6 Roll 6)**: $-3.0$ Tension, $+8.0$ Energy, `dice_legendary` seed.

## Wild Magic Cascades
After each manual roll (at depth 0), `/dice` can invoke another pantheon skill (e.g. `/story`, `/card`, `/pkm`, `/poem`, `/joke`) depending on the roll's tier band.
