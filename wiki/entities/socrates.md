# Socrates

Type: PERSON

## From [cybernetics-and-mythos-the-architecture-of-intell-part2](/entities/cybernetics-and-mythos-the-architecture-of-intell-part2.md) (2026-06-08)
- Presented by Plato as the relentless and curious interrogator of prominent Athenian citizens.
- His method of questioning is known as the Socratic elenchus.
- Sought to define real knowledge as 'knowledge which knows itself to be ignorance'.
- The dialectic originated as the aporetic Socratic tool for dismantling false assumptions and cultivating foundational intellectual humility.

## From [drive-research-dialectics-socratic-vs-hegelian-methods](/entities/drive-research-dialectics-socratic-vs-hegelian-methods.md) (2026-06-08)
- Depicted as a relentless and curious interrogator in Plato's early dialogues.
- Used the elenchus, a method of cross-examination focused on moral and epistemological issues.
- Sought to expose ignorance and establish intellectual humility.
