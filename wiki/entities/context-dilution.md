# Context Dilution

Type: CONCEPT

## From [[ai-research-part6-micro04|ai-research-part6-micro04]] (2026-06-09)
- Problem with monolithic models
- Signal strength of important instructions collapses in noise
- Also referred to as 'PreNorm Dilution'

## From [[ai-research-part6-micro05|ai-research-part6-micro05]] (2026-06-09)
- A problem with monolithic agent models.
- Leads to lack of specialization.

## From [[drive-research-redefining-agentic-soulmd-to-dialog-micro02|drive-research-redefining-agentic-soulmd-to-dialog-micro02]] (2026-06-09)
- A phenomenon where initial prompts become a minuscule fraction of the total context window in extended conversations.
- Leads to the initial instructions being outweighed by subsequent dialogue.
- A vulnerability of the static SOUL.md paradigm.
