Type: SYSTEM

## From [drive-research-linux-gaming-and-ai-build-guide-micro02](/entities/drive-research-linux-gaming-and-ai-build-guide-micro02.md) (2026-06-09)
- Requires a native PCIe 5.0 NVMe SSD architecture.
