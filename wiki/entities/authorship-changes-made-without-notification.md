# authorship changes made without notification

Type: CONCEPT

## From [[drive-research-scientific-writing-and-publication-process-micro03|drive-research-scientific-writing-and-publication-process-micro03]] (2026-06-09)
- Designation holds immense academic, social, and financial implications.
- Defining authorship and order is the collective responsibility of the research team.
- Failing to list an individual who meaningfully contributed to the work.
- A severe academic misconduct offense.
- Editors do not arbitrate these; they must be investigated by home institutions.
- Including a highly respected senior figure who did not contribute, merely to artificially inflate the manuscript's prestige.
- A warning sign of authorship problems.
- Adding a colleague out of personal obligation, appreciation, or reciprocal favor despite a lack of intellectual contribution.
