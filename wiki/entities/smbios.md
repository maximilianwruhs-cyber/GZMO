# SMBIOS

Type: CONCEPT

## From [[drive-research-automating-linux-hardware-detection-micro01|drive-research-automating-linux-hardware-detection-micro01]] (2026-06-09)
- Specification maintained by the Distributed Management Task Force (DMTF).
- Provides a globally standardized mechanism for motherboard and system vendors to format and present comprehensive hardware management telemetry.
- Structured as a sequence of highly specific tables that reside within system memory.

## From [[drive-research-automating-linux-hardware-detection-micro03|drive-research-automating-linux-hardware-detection-micro03]] (2026-06-09)
- Records are utilized to grant the kernel in-band access to telemetry.
- Standardized interfaces of SMBIOS tables bridge the gap between firmware and kernel.
