# feedback loop

Type: CONCEPT

## From [[cybernetics-and-mythos-the-architecture-of-intell-part1|cybernetics-and-mythos-the-architecture-of-intell-part1]] (2026-06-08)
- At the absolute center of any cybernetic system lies the feedback loop.
- It is the precise mechanism by which a system measures its own behavior, compares it to a predetermined goal state, and adjusts its future actions accordingly.
- It requires a highly structured, recursive architecture capable of sensing dynamic changes, processing those changes against an established paradigm, and enacting physical or digital alterations.
