# SIGKILL

Type: CONCEPT

## From [[drive-research-architecting-a-linux-task-manager-design-principl|drive-research-architecting-a-linux-task-manager-design-principl]] (2026-06-08)
- Signal ID: 9.
- Kill signal.
- Forceful, uncompromising termination; the kernel destroys the process immediately, with no cleanup routines executed.
- Cannot be ignored.
