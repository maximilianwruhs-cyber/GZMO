# effector sharing

## From [[cybernetics-and-mythos-the-architecture-of-intell-part1|cybernetics-and-mythos-the-architecture-of-intell-part1]] (2026-06-08)
- A critical phenomenon observed in complex cybernetic entities where multiple sensor-effector systems utilize the same actuator to achieve distinct regulatory goals.
- Extends the range of system control and allows a system to maintain homeostasis even if a primary effector fails.
- Introduces systemic vulnerabilities, where a severe disturbance affecting one regulated variable can force another into a new, potentially dangerous steady-state value.
- Executes the necessary changes to adjust the variable.
- Acts upon the environment or the system itself to reduce the error signal, often serving as an amplifier converting signal units into physical effects.
- Examples include sweat glands cooling the body and the mechanical relay turning on a heating element.
