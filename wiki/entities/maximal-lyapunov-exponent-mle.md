# Maximal Lyapunov Exponent (MLE)

Type: CONCEPT

## From [[drive-research-algorithmic-trading-with-chaos-theory|drive-research-algorithmic-trading-with-chaos-theory]] (2026-06-08)
- Determines the overall predictability of a system.
- If positive, nearby trajectories diverge exponentially.
- Used to calculate the Lyapunov time.

## From [[drive-research-cache-optimization-with-ai-chaos-theory|drive-research-cache-optimization-with-ai-chaos-theory]] (2026-06-08)
- Measures the average exponential rate at which infinitesimally close trajectories separate in phase space over time.
- A positive MLE confirms deterministic chaos, dictating a strict 'predictability horizon'.
- Acts as a continuous barometer for potential risk.
