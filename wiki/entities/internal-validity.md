# Internal Validity

Type: CONCEPT

## From [[the-architecture-of-scientific-inquiry-and-academi|the-architecture-of-scientific-inquiry-and-academi]] (2026-06-08)
- Measures the degree of confidence that a tested causal relationship is trustworthy.
- Not influenced by extraneous variables or systematic biases.
- Asks whether the experimental treatment truly makes a difference.

## From [[drive-research-die-architektur-der-wissenschaftlichen-forschung|drive-research-die-architektur-der-wissenschaftlichen-forschung]] (2026-06-08)
- Measures the extent of confidence that a tested causal relationship is trustworthy.
- Assesses whether the experimental treatment truly makes a difference.
- Is maximized in highly controlled laboratory environments.
