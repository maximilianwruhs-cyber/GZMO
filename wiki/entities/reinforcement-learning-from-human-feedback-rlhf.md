## From [[cybernetics-and-mythos-the-architecture-of-intell-part1|cybernetics-and-mythos-the-architecture-of-intell-part1]] (2026-06-08)

- Models trained with RLHF exhibit a reflexive tendency to follow the latest instruction.
- This training can lead to abandoning assigned personas mid-conversation.
