- A process where the outcome of an event reinforces the inciting process, thereby building momentum and exacerbating the effects of a small disturbance.
- Generally drives a system toward instability, exponential growth, increasing oscillations, chaotic behavior, or severe divergences from equilibrium.
- Examples include normal childbirth driven by oxytocin, the Larsen effect in audio engineering, and network effects in economics.
