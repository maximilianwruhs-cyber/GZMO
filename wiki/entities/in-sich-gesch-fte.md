# In-sich-Geschäfte

Type: CONCEPT

## From [[drive-research-setting-up-autonomous-trading-agents-micro02|drive-research-setting-up-autonomous-trading-agents-micro02]] (2026-06-09)
- Referred to as 'wash trading' in the DACH region.
- Occurs when an investor's buy and sell orders inadvertently match against each other on the exchange's order book.
- Creates a false and misleading impression of market activity and liquidity.
