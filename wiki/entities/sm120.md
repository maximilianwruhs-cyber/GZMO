# SM120

Type: SYSTEM

## From [[drive-research-imagine-creating-sm120-according-to-our-progress|drive-research-imagine-creating-sm120-according-to-our-progress]] (2026-06-08)
- Workstation-class Blackwell hardware.
- Consumer accelerators introduce critical hardware constraints and software integration traps.
- Block allocations are capped at a maximum of 99 KiB of shared memory.
- Physical TMEM is entirely absent.
- Relies on traditional warp-synchronous, extended mma.sync instructions.
- Paired with a GDDR7 memory bus.
- Reports a physical compute capability of exactly 12.0.
- Introduces Cluster Launch Control (CLC) for hardware-supported work-stealing.

## From [[drive-research-marlin-baseline-for-early-deployments-micro01|drive-research-marlin-baseline-for-early-deployments-micro01]] (2026-06-10)

- Includes RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Workstation Edition and RTX 5090.
- Constrained to 99 KiB of shared memory and lacks physical TMEM.
