# Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD)

Type: CONCEPT

## From [[drive-research-ai-agentic-teacher|drive-research-ai-agentic-teacher]] (2026-06-08)
- The AI adapts to the student's ZPD.
- Involves phases of High Support, Guided Support, and Withdrawal/Fading.

## From [[drive-research-ai-agentic-teacher-research-report|drive-research-ai-agentic-teacher-research-report]] (2026-06-08)
- A concept from Lev Vygotsky’s sociocultural theory.
- Represents the cognitive space between what a learner can achieve independently and with guidance.
- Agentic systems operationalize the ZPD through the Evidence-Decision-Feedback (EDF) framework.
