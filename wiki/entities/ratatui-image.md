## From [[drive-research-rust-tui-architecture-tech-stack1-micro04|drive-research-rust-tui-architecture-tech-stack1-micro04]] (2026-06-09)
- Definitive crate for implementing native graphics protocols in TUIs.
- Unified abstraction layer over three primary terminal image protocols.
- Utilizes a Picker helper to detect terminal capabilities.
- Supports Sixel, Kitty Graphics, iTerm2 Protocols, and Unicode Halfblocks.
- Definitive, state-of-the-art framework for building TUIs in Rust.
- Community-driven fork and continuation of tui-rs.
- Cornerstone of the Rust terminal ecosystem.
- Operates fundamentally on an immediate-mode rendering paradigm paired with double-buffering.
- Offers unparalleled performance and unopinionated architectural flexibility.
