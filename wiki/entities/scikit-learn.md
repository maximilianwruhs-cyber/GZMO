## From [drive-research-free-ai-and-cs-self-study-roadmap](/entities/drive-research-free-ai-and-cs-self-study-roadmap.md) (2026-06-08)

- provides an incredibly elegant, uniform Application Programming Interface (API) for data preprocessing, model fitting, and statistical evaluation
- abstracts the heavy mathematics into simple .fit() and .predict() methods
- official Scikit-Learn 'Getting Started' documentation is an exceptionally well-written text-based resource
- exhaustive, multi-hour crash courses focused purely on Scikit-Learn are available entirely for free on YouTube channels such as FreeCodeCamp
