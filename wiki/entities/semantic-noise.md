# semantic noise

## From [[cybernetics-and-mythos-the-architecture-of-intell-part1|cybernetics-and-mythos-the-architecture-of-intell-part1]] (2026-06-08)
- Refers to the perturbations or distortions of meaning that occur when a message is successfully received on a physical level, but is fundamentally misinterpreted conceptually.
- Occurs because the semantic capacities, cultural context, and statistical expectations of the receiver may not match the intentions of the transmitter.
- In social cybernetics, it becomes the primary obstacle in the information loop.
