- Defined by Heinz von Foerster as the 'cybernetics of observing systems'.
- The observer is inextricably embedded within the system.
- Requires abandoning the illusion of the detached observer.
- The agent must account for its own cognitive processes and biases.
- The transdisciplinary study of circular causal processes, feedback loops, recursion, and information communication.
- Derived from the Greek term 'kybernetes' (steersman or navigator).
