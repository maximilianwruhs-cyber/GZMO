# Material Spectral Gaps

Type: CONCEPT

## From [dynamics-of-the-unpredictable-micro04](/entities/dynamics-of-the-unpredictable-micro04.md) (2026-06-09)
- Calculating the spectral gap of a material is equivalent to the halting problem and therefore universally undecidable.
- This was proven in 2015 and expanded upon in 2024.
- The spectral gap is the energy required to move a macroscopic system out of its lowest energy state under magnetic fields.
