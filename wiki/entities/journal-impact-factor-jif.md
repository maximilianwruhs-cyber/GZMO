# Journal Impact Factor (JIF)

Type: CONCEPT

## From [[the-architecture-of-scientific-inquiry-and-academi|the-architecture-of-scientific-inquiry-and-academi]] (2026-06-08)
- Historically the primary metric for journal prestige.
- Calculates the average number of citations received by articles in a journal over a specific period.
- Relying solely on JIF is heavily discouraged by publication ethics bodies.
