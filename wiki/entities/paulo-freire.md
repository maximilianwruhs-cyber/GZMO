- The Brazilian educator Paulo Freire explicitly synthesized Hegelian and Marxist dialectics to formulate a revolutionary approach to adult education.
- In his seminal work, Pedagogy of the Oppressed (1970), Freire utilized the exact Hegelian distinction between a "being in-itself" and a "being for-itself" to describe the psychological and social trajectory of marginalized populations.
- Freire’s pedagogy requires individuals to engage in a dialectical process.
