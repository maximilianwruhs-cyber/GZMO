Type: HARDWARE

## From [[drive-research-ultimate-linux-workstation-tuning-blueprint|drive-research-ultimate-linux-workstation-tuning-blueprint]] (2026-06-08)

- Supports DDR5 memory.
- platform for DDR5 overclocking
