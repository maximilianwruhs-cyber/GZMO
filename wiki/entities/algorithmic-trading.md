## From [[drive-research-setting-up-autonomous-trading-agents-micro02|drive-research-setting-up-autonomous-trading-agents-micro02]] (2026-06-09)
- Defined under MiFID II.
- Entails assuming total legal responsibility for the actions, errors, and market impacts of the underlying algorithm.
- Requires rigorous compliance with the EU Market Abuse Regulation (MAR).

- Subject to MiFID II and MAR regulations.
- Can inadvertently trigger market-disrupting events.
