## Field A — Utility-aware retrieve (our ship)

**Still the paper:** [MemRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03192) — Intent–Experience–Utility triplets; two-phase retrieve (semantic filter → Q-select); Monte Carlo utility update; frozen LLM.

**What they have that we already copied in spirit:** `honeypot.utility_score`, `reinforce_by`, `ORDER BY utility_score` (G4, July).

**What they have that we do not:** an explicit **reward from task outcome** (ALFWorld / BigCodeBench success). Our reinforce is graded felt-use (glance/cited/bonded), not environment return. That is a *feature* for a Keep (human work, not a game) and a *gap* if we never close the loop from “this recall helped the next session.”

**Steal next (telescope-legal):** define a fail-closed **outcome hook** — e.g. operator marks a recall useful, or a later takeaway cites the fact — and bump Q only then. Do not invent rewards from TDP or from search count.

**Memento** ([arXiv 2508.16153](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16153), [github.com/Agent-on-the-Fly/Memento](https://github.com/Agent-on-the-Fly/Memento)): memory-augmented MDP, case bank, retrieve-by-value, no weight updates. Planner–executor + MCP. Steal: store **failures** as well as wins. Non-steal: cloud executor as the product path.

## Field C — Intentional forgetting

SleepGate and SCM both treat forgetting as **signal-to-noise**, not decay-as-apology. Immune (`gzmo immune forget` / value plan) is our organ.

**Steal:** conflict-aware temporal tag (new fact supersedes old — we have supersession chains; make the *gate* emit a typed refuse/supersede).  
**Non-steal:** attention-entropy over KV as a living KPI.

**Thought.** A Keep that only remembers is a hoard. The papers are finally saying that in public. We said it in July. Stay ahead by *applying* forget on CT101, not by renaming immune.
